MONKEY INC BOT: PLAYER GUIDE
=============================

Monkey Inc keeps track of which CQ squads you can make each week. Everything happens through
slash commands (type / in Discord) and buttons. Only you can see the bot's replies to you.

Availability levels
-------------------
  🟢 Preferred       You want to run this squad
  🟡 Available       You can make it if needed
  🔴 Not Available   You can't make it

Squad times are always shown in YOUR timezone.

Default schedule vs. weekly availability
----------------------------------------
- Your DEFAULT schedule is your usual availability for every squad. The hosts use it
  whenever you haven't said otherwise.
- WEEKLY availability overrides your default for one week only. Use it when your plans
  change for a particular week. You can submit it for next week, or up to 7 weeks after
  that if you already know you'll be busy (the "Update a future week" button in /cq availability).
- A new week starts on Sunday. "Next week" always means the week starting on the coming Sunday.


1. THE WEEKLY REMINDER (most players only need this)
----------------------------------------------------
Every week the bot posts a reminder in the CQ channel (by default on Wednesday and again on
Friday) with the squad times and the deadline.
Press one of its buttons:

  📋 View my availability   Shows YOUR availability for that week, visible only to you: your
                            change for that week if you made one, otherwise your default.
  ✅ No change              Your default schedule works this week. That's it, you're done.
  ✏️ Change weekly availability
                            Opens the availability pop-up so you can set different
                            availability for just this week.

"View my availability" (and /cq availability) shows your squads grouped into 🟢 Preferred,
🟡 Available and 🔴 Not Available, with a count for each. The top line tells you whether
you've checked in for the week. If you changed that week, a "Different from your default"
list shows which squads you changed (e.g. Squad 1: 🟢 → 🔴).

Please press either "No change" or "Change weekly availability" before the deadline, so the
hosts know you've checked in. You can press them again later to change your answer.


2. CHANGING A FUTURE WEEK
-------------------------
The 🗓️ "Update a future week" button in /cq availability first asks WHICH week
you want to change. It lists next week and the 7 weeks after it, by date:

  ▫️ Week of Sunday Oct 4, 2026   Next week · Default schedule
  ✏️ Week of Sunday Oct 11, 2026  In 2 weeks · Changed

  - Choose a week from "Choose a week to change…" to open the availability pop-up for that week.
  - ✏️ marks weeks you've already changed. Pick one again to edit it.
  - "Reset a week to your default…" undoes a change: that week goes back to your
    default schedule.

Changing a future week counts as your check-in for that week, so you won't need to respond
to that week's reminder (you still can if your plans change again).


3. THE AVAILABILITY POP-UP
--------------------------
"Change weekly availability", the week you pick with "Update a future week", "Change default
availability", and the character you pick with "Change a character's week" or "Character
availability" all open the same pop-up:

  - The first time, it asks for your timezone (pick it from the list). Change it any time in
    /settings.
  - Each checkbox shows the run and its time in your timezone, e.g.
    "Squad 7 · Sat Oct 10, 9:00 PM". Runs are listed in your squad order (see "Squad order"
    below).
  - Tick the runs you'd most like under 🟢 Preferred and the others you can make under
    🟡 Available. Discord fits at most 10 checkboxes in a group, so with more runs each
    section is split in two: "(1 of 2)" and "(2 of 2)".
  - Runs you leave unticked in both sections are saved as Not Available. A run ticked in both
    is saved as Preferred.
  - Can't make it at all? Tick "❌ Can't make any of these runs" at the top: every run is
    saved as Not Available, whatever else is ticked.
  - Your current choices start out ticked. Press Submit to save, or close the pop-up to
    change nothing.
  - For a character, each run also shows what it is otherwise, e.g. "Your default: 🟢 Preferred"
    (Character availability) or "Usual this week: 🟡 Available" (Change a character's week).
    Only the runs you set differently are saved for that character.

If hosts ever set up more than 20 runs, they won't fit in the pop-up, and these buttons open
a page-by-page editor instead: a dropdown per squad, 4 per page, ◀ ▶ to change page, then
Save. It stays open for 15 minutes.


4. COMMANDS
-----------
There are four commands: /cq availability, /cq characters, /settings and /findatime.
Everything else is a button on them.

  /cq availability          Show your availability for next week (same view as the reminder's
                            "View my availability"), grouped by 🟢 / 🟡 / 🔴, with whether
                            you've checked in at the top and any later weeks you've changed.
                            First row of buttons:
                              No change / Checked in / Use my default
                                  Not checked in yet: a green "No change" (your default works
                                  for the week). Once you're checked in it turns grey: "Checked
                                  in", or "Use my default" if you changed the week (press it to
                                  undo your changes for that week).
                              Change weekly availability
                                  change your availability for the week shown
                              Change a character's week
                                  change one character's availability for the week shown only,
                                  e.g. your DRK can't make Saturday this week. It's back to
                                  normal the week after. Counts as checking in.
                              Update a future week
                                  change next week or up to 7 weeks ahead (you pick the week
                                  first; see section 2)
                            Second row:
                              🔀 Sort by squad # / time
                              Change default availability
                                  change your usual schedule, used for every week you don't
                                  change

  /cq characters            List your characters (status, job, buff, damage, squad). Damage
                            is the average of your recent runs from the hosts' damage logs.
                            It also says who to message to add new characters.
                            Buttons:
                              Set character status    mark characters static or flex
                                                      (see below)
                              Character availability  give one character an ongoing schedule
                                                      that differs from your default, e.g. you
                                                      can only bring your DRK on weekends (every
                                                      week, until you change it)
                              Damage history          pick a character to see its damage
                                                      from the hosts' damage logs: its last
                                                      15 runs (★ = counted in its average)

  /settings                 Your bot settings, visible only to you:
                              🌐 Timezone     used for the run times in the availability
                                              pop-up's checkboxes and in /findatime
                              🔀 Squad order  list squads by time or by squad number
                                              everywhere (the same as the 🔀 buttons)

  /findatime [title]        Start a poll to find a time when several people are free, for any
                            boss (see "Finding a time" below). The poll is posted in the
                            channel for everyone.

Character status
  Each character is one of:
    ⭐ Static     Prioritize this character when slotting.
    ⏳ Flex       Only slot this character if needed.
    💤 Inactive   Not slotted, and hidden from the hosts' squad lists. Only a host can
                  set this; ask a host if a character should be (or stop being) inactive.
  Press "Set character status" in /cq characters to choose Static or Flex for each
  character. It opens an editor: a dropdown per character, 4 per page, ◀ ▶ to change
  page, then Save. Nothing changes until you press Save.
  Inactive characters are shown there but can't be changed.
  Characters marked Perm on the roster sheet start as Static; the rest start as Flex.

Squad order
  Every list of squads starts in time order (the order they run in the week). Press 🔀
  "Sort by squad #" on any of them to sort by squad number instead. The bot remembers your
  choice and uses it everywhere: your availability and the availability pop-up. You can
  also change it in /settings. (The reminder in the
  channel is shared by everyone, so it always lists squads in time order; press "View my
  availability" on it to see them in your order.) Press 🔀 "Sort by time" to switch back.

Joining the roster, and adding, changing or removing characters, is done by the hosts.
Ask a host if you're new, have a new character, or something on /cq characters is wrong.


Finding a time (/findatime)
  Anyone can start a poll, e.g. /findatime title:Lotus hard. It covers the 7 days from when it
  was started and lists everyone who has joined. The poll's buttons work for everyone:
    ✋ Join/Edit     Type when you're free, in plain English and in your own time, e.g.
                    "Fri 5-7pm, Sat after 2pm", "weekdays 8-11pm", "Sun all day",
                    "Mon-Wed 9pm-1am", "tomorrow evening" or "10/12 6-9pm". Separate parts with
                    commas. A part with only a time uses the day before it ("Fri 1-3pm,
                    6-9pm"); a day with no time means all day. Times without am/pm are read as
                    PM (you're told). A day name means the next one that's still ahead for
                    you: if it's Friday evening and you type "Fri 3-6pm", that's next Friday.
                    Hours that have already passed, or come after the poll ends, are left out
                    (you're told).
                    The bot shows your free hours for each day. In 🖥️ Grid view it's one
                    square per hour (🟩 free, ⬛ not, ⬜ already passed),
                    in four labelled groups: 12-6AM, 6-12PM, 12-6PM, 6-12AM. It also lists
                    anything it couldn't read. Press ✅ Confirm to save it, or ✏️ Edit to fix it.
                    Already joined? Join/Edit shows your hours (only to you) with ✏️ Edit and
                    ➖ Leave poll.
    🗓️ See schedule  Everyone's hours for one day at a time (pick the day, or ◀ ▶), in your
                    timezone. Hours that have passed are left out (⬜ in Grid view). Only you
                    see it.
    ⭐ Best time     The times still ahead when the most people are free together, longest and
                    soonest first, with who's missing. If no time works for 2+ people, it says
                    so (and mentions any overlap that has already passed). Only you see it.
  The poll's creator (and hosts) also get controls under Best time, only they see them:
    📣 Propose a time: a pop-up lists the best times again (in your time), then you type when the
       run should start, e.g. "tue 1pm" (or a range, "tue 1-3pm"). The bot shows how it read it;
       press 📣 Send, or ✏️ Edit to fix it. Send posts the proposal in the channel and pings
       everyone who joined. Each person answers with ✅ Confirm,
       ❌ Can't make it, or 🔁 Propose different time (typed like Join); the proposal message
       shows everyone's answers and who hasn't answered yet. Only people in the poll can
       answer, and you can change your answer.
    🔒 Close poll: no new joins. See schedule and Best time still work.
  Hours are shown in words at first (e.g. "Robin · 5 PM-7 PM"), with a 🤝 line in See schedule for
  when 2+ people are free; this reads best on phones. Press 🖥️ Grid view (on See schedule, the Join
  preview or your hours) for the squares instead; 📱 Compact view switches back. Each new message
  starts in words.
  Your timezone comes from /settings; the first Join asks for it if it isn't set.


5. FIRST TIME USING THE BOT?
----------------------------
If you were already on the CQ roster sheet, you're already in the bot with your
characters and default schedule. The first time you press a button or run a command, the
bot links you by your Discord username.

- If the bot says it can't find you, your Discord username has probably changed since
  the roster was made. Ask a host to link you (/player > Link).
- If you're brand new, ask a host to add you and your characters. Then set your usual
  availability with "Change default availability" in /cq availability.


FAQ
---
Q: I pressed "No change" but then my plans changed.
A: Press "Change weekly availability" (or "Update a future week" in /cq availability) and save your new availability. Your
   latest answer is the one that counts.

Q: I submitted a weekly update by mistake and want my default back.
A: For next week, press "Use my default" in /cq availability (it also undoes character changes
   for that week). For any week, press "Update a future week" and pick the week under
   "Reset a week to your default…".

Q: I'm away in three weeks. Can I tell the hosts now?
A: Yes. Press "Update a future week" in /cq availability, choose that week, and set it to Not Available.

Q: Does updating my default change a week I've already submitted?
A: No. A weekly update always wins for that week. Your default applies to weeks where you
   haven't submitted one.
